Mobile app (iOS & Android)

  • Premium / subscriptions: Managed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Subscribe, restore, or cancel in your device’s subscription settings. Subscriptions must be configured in App Store Connect and Play Console with the correct product IDs.
  • StoreKit “failed to get response”: Common on the iOS Simulator or before subscription products exist in App Store Connect. Test on a real device with a sandbox account or TestFlight build when possible.
  • Microphone & storage: Grant permissions in system Settings if recording or import/export does not work.

Web app

  • Click the page once if audio does not start (browser autoplay rules).
  • Use a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) with JavaScript enabled.
